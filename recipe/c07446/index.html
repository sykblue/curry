What are the essential ingredients for preparing a Thai butternut squash curry?

The essential ingredients for a Thai butternut squash curry include 10 ml of vegetable oil, 1 tbsp of Thai red curry paste, a 2-cm piece of fresh ginger (finely chopped or grated), 2 cloves of garlic (peeled and finely chopped or crushed), 3 medium-sized onions (peeled and finely chopped), 1 red pepper (de-seeded and finely chopped), 1 butternut squash (peeled, de-seeded and diced), 500 ml of water, 400 g tin of low-fat coconut milk, 400 g tin of drained chickpeas, 1 bunch of fresh coriander (chopped), and 1 lime (juiced).
