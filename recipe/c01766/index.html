What are suitable substitutions for common Asian cooking ingredients?

For 1 tablespoon of fish sauce, use 1/2 tablespoon anchovy paste, 1/2 teaspoon soy sauce, and 1 teaspoon stock or broth. For 1 tablespoon Hoisin sauce, substitute 1/2 tablespoon lite soy sauce, 1 teaspoon sugar, and 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger. Mirin can be substituted with any sweet white wine or a generic white wine with a pinch of sugar. Rice vinegar can be replaced with white wine vinegar. Asian hot chili sauce can be substituted with regular chili sauce. For 1 tablespoon green curry paste, substitute 1/2 tablespoon yellow curry powder thinned with 1-2 teaspoons water. For 1 tablespoon fresh ginger root, substitute 1 teaspoon ginger powder. Fresh ginger root can also be stored in the freezer for up to 1 year and grated as needed. Anchovy paste can be replaced with an equal amount of mashed canned anchovy fillets.
