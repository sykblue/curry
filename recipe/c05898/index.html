What are the essential ingredients for preparing Coronation chicken?

The essential ingredients for Coronation chicken include 4 lean chicken fillets (around 520g), 10ml of vegetable oil, 1 small onion (peeled and finely chopped), 1 tbsp of curry powder, 150ml of water, 1 heaped tsp of tomato puree, 1 x 125g pot of low-fat natural yoghurt, and black pepper to taste.
