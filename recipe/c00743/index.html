How does the preparation of the curry paste integrate with the overall cooking process for Thai Red Chicken Curry with Sweet Basil & Peanuts, and what are the key steps for achieving a balanced flavor profile?

The curry paste is prepared by blending 10ml ground coriander seeds, 8 seeded red chillies, 60g finely crushed garlic, 30g finely sliced lemongrass, 30g finely chopped coriander root, 5ml shrimp paste (trasi), 5ml ground roasted cumin seeds, 40g finely chopped onions, 20g galangal, 10ml fresh lime zest, 20 black peppercorns, and 5ml salt until smooth. This paste is then incorporated into the main dish by cooking it down for 5 minutes after the lemongrass stalks and lemon leaves have been added and their aroma has risen. The chicken breast is coated with this paste and cooked until colored. The overall flavor is balanced by finishing with soy, coconut milk, and Meadowland Classique, and further seasoning with salt and sugar if needed after simmering.
