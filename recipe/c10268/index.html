What are the recommended garnishes and serving suggestions for the Pan Fried Chicken Schnitzel with Thai Red Curry Sauce & Jasmine Rice?

The dish is recommended to be garnished with fresh coriander, some chopped toasted peanuts, and extra lime wedges. For serving, spoon jasmine rice into each serving bowl, top with bok choy and sugar snap peas, then add the sliced chicken schnitzel. The Thai red curry sauce can be poured over the schnitzel or served on the side.
