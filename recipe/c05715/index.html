What are the essential ingredients for preparing a Thai Green Curry?

The essential ingredients for preparing a Thai Green Curry are 2 chicken breasts, 1 can of coconut milk (or low-fat coconut milk), 1 onion, 1 aubergine, 1 chopped green chilli, and Thai Green Curry Paste.
