What are the common ingredients and cooking methods for the three appetizer recipes presented?

All three appetizer recipes—scrambled eggs with cheese and tomatoes, Japanese scallion baked eggs, and mackerel with curry-fried cabbage—utilize one standard-sized egg. The cooking method for all three involves whisking the egg and then heating the mixture in a microwave at 600W for approximately 1 minute and 10 seconds, with the mackerel dish requiring 1 minute and 20 seconds. Each dish is designed for a single serving.
